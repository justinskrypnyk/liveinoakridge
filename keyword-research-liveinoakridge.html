Read this before the tables

DataForSEO groups near-identical phrasings under a shared core keyword and reports the same volume against every variant. Adding those variants together would inflate the numbers badly. The eight ways of writing "houses for sale oakridge london ontario" all return 390 with identical month-by-month histories, so the cluster is worth 390, not 3,120. Every total in this report counts each core once, and the variant counts are stated so you can see what was collapsed.

The endpoint also returns a search intent label for most keywords. That label is shown as supplied. Where it contradicts what the live SERP actually returns, the row is flagged as reclassified and the reason is given. The endpoint labels most neighbourhood inventory queries "navigational", which does not match a SERP full of MLS inventory pages, so those sit in the transactional group here.

6. Where the top 10 looks weak

Five SERPs were pulled live at London, Ontario on 2 August 2026 before any claim below was made. Clusters not checked are marked "not checked" and carry no weakness claim.

I1 London Ontario neighbourhoods: weak — and the site already has an unused answer

Position 7 is a dental practice writing about neighbourhoods. Position 5 is a Reddit thread asking whether a good neighbourhood map exists. Position 2 is a community non-profit directory and position 3 is a City of London census page. No brokerage holds a strong editorial position on page one. The AI overview on this query cites seven separate realtor sites, none of which rank organically in the top four, which means Google is pulling neighbourhood expertise from agents but has no single authoritative agent page to rank.

Corrected, with a caveat This is the clearest gap in the set — but the site has held a page built exactly for it since April 2026: /blog/london-ontario-neighbourhood-guide-2026/, 1,072 words, indexed, and already linked from the footer plus at least seven other blog posts with anchor text like "complete London Ontario neighbourhood guide." Unlike the realtor page in the companion report, this one is not starved of internal links — and it still shows zero Search Console impressions for this term in 90 days. That rules out the easy fix. What's left is either a genuine authority gap on a weak-but-not-empty SERP, or a title/content mismatch worth a closer look (the page targets "neighbourhood guide," singular-ish phrasing; the query is "neighbourhoods," plural) — not something to guess at further without checking rankings directly in a few weeks.

8. Build order — rewritten 2 August 2026

The original build order was seven steps of "build this page," priced at the cost of a new URL each. With 12 of 15 clusters already live, the real order is shorter and mostly about fixing why existing pages are invisible, not writing new ones.

  1. ✓ Done — Oakridge, Byron, Westmount area pages: stop looking like duplicates

    All three already had live inventory, price ranges and full guides. Their H1s and title tags didn't match real searcher phrasing and, for Oakridge specifically, duplicated the homepage's own H1 almost word for word. Reworded all three (and the shared West London/Whitehills template) to "Homes for Sale in [Area], London Ontario," and re-pointed the homepage's Oakridge CTA at the area page instead of the generic search tool.

    Covers 3,950 searches/mo (Oakridge + Byron + Westmount inventory and name terms combined).

  2. ✓ Done — Cross-link the three neighbourhood comparison posts to their area guides

    The Oakridge-vs-Byron, Riverbend-vs-Byron, and Westmount-vs-West-London posts already linked to their area pages repeatedly, but their titles ran 93–103 characters with the site suffix and were truncating in the SERP. Shortened all three titles and added an explicit "already know which one you want? Read the full guide" line near the top of each.

    No single keyword — cleans up SERP presentation on ~4,900 searches/mo of comparison-intent queries.

  3. ✓ Done — Link /blog/london-ontario-realtor/ from somewhere

    Existed since 20 July, zero internal links anywhere on the site. Added links from the homepage and footer. See the companion Site Explorer report for the full writeup.

    2,920 searches/mo.

  4. Figure out why /blog/london-ontario-neighbourhood-guide-2026/ is invisible despite being well-linked

    This is the one page on the site that does NOT fit the "just add internal links" pattern — it already has links from the footer and at least seven other posts, and still shows zero impressions for "london ontario neighbourhoods" in 90 days. Before writing a new page here, check its actual ranking position directly (not yet done) and consider whether the title's exact phrasing ("neighbourhood guide" vs. "neighbourhoods") or a genuine authority gap is the blocker.

    230 searches/mo. Needs a rankings check, not a rebuild — yet.

  5. Consolidate the monthly market posts into one evergreen /london-ontario-housing-market/ URL

    The content already exists — May, June and July 2026 updates plus a months-of-supply post and a weekly digest — but it's split across five URLs instead of concentrated on one that gets updated. A single RE/MAX agent blog already holds position 4 on this SERP, so a working broker can rank here. This is a real, sized content project: decide whether to fold the dated posts into one page or keep them as an archive linking to a current evergreen hub.

    530 searches/mo. SERP checked: moderate.

  6. Check why /home-value-estimate/ has never been crawled by Google

    Confirmed via Search Console: "URL is unknown to Google," despite being linked from the homepage and every area page and sitting in the sitemap. Cheapest possible fix on this entire list — request indexing for it directly in the Search Console UI (not available via API) and see if it gets picked up within a couple of weeks.

    Not a keyword volume play — a basic technical gap on a tool that should already be indexed.

  7. Then: a broader "moving to London Ontario" guide, citywide inventory

    The existing Toronto-specific moving guide doesn't fully cover the general "moving to/cost of living in London Ontario" intent (30–170/mo combined) — genuinely worth a page if there's time, but low urgency. The citywide inventory cluster at 29,500 stays deliberately last: with 13 referring domains, competing with realtor.ca and zolo.ca on "houses for sale london ontario" is not a twelve-month proposition, and the same effort spent fixing existing West London pages produces actual clients sooner.

    Combined ~600 searches/mo, plus 29,500 deferred.

What was unavailable, and why

  • Keyword Difficulty on most local terms. The API returns difficulty only where it holds a value. It was absent for 24 of the 55 kept keywords, including every neighbourhood inventory term. Those cells read "not available" rather than carrying an estimate. Competition level and average referring domains for the SERP were used instead where difficulty was missing.
  • Three requested keywords returned no data at all. "sell my house london ontario", "home evaluation london ontario" and "sifton bog london ontario" were submitted to the keyword overview endpoint and omitted from the response, meaning the database holds no record of them. "sifton bog" without the city qualifier does return data, at 3,600.
  • Three more returned no volume. "how much is my house worth london ontario", "west london ontario homes for sale" and "oakridge secondary school" came back with an intent label but no keyword_info block, so volume and difficulty are printed as not available.
  • City-level volume is not available. The Labs keyword endpoints accept country-level locations only, so every volume figure here is Canada-wide. For terms containing "london ontario" the practical difference is small, but the citywide figures should be read as national totals for those phrases. The live SERP calls were run at London, Ontario.
  • Ten clusters have no SERP check. Only five SERPs were pulled. No weakness claim is made for the other ten, and they are marked "not checked" in the cluster table rather than guessed at.

Confidence notes

  • Build step 6, the realtor service page, is labelled low confidence on timing. The keyword is achievable at difficulty 16, but the domain currently holds 13 referring domains and two ranking keywords.   • The de-duplicated volume figures depend on the judgement that identical month-by-month histories across phrasing variants indicate one shared underlying volume. DataForSEO does not state this explicitly. If that reading is wrong, the totals here are conservative rather than inflated.
  • The seed you chose, "oakridge london ontario real estate", is itself a weak term at 20 searches per month with a yearly trend of minus 80 percent. The expansion above deliberately moves off it toward the phrasings that carry volume.
