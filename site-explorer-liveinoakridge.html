Where the site stands

This is a young domain. The backlink index first saw it on 9 May 2025, and after roughly fifteen months it holds two organic keyword positions in Canada, both outside the top 20, both pointing at the homepage. Estimated organic traffic is under one click per month. Everything below is written on that basis.

Correction, 2 August 2026: three "build this page" recommendations described pages that already exist

The original version of this report (Section 4 and the action list) told Justin to build three new URLs — /london-ontario-realtor/, /oakridge-homes-for-sale/, and /oakridge/ — on the reasoning that nothing ranks for those terms, so no page must exist. That inference was wrong. All three pages already exist, are live, and are indexed by Google:

  • /blog/london-ontario-realtor/ — published 20 July 2026, covers credentials, process, and what to look for in a realtor. Indexed with Review-snippet rich results.
  • /areas/oakridge/ — already the site's best-built page for both Oakridge terms: live MLS listing count and price range above the fold, sub-neighbourhood nav, schools, market stats, full history. Indexed since at least 12 July 2026.
  • /areas/oakridge/buy/ — a "why buy here" service page, live but not a listings page in its own right.

Verified two ways: a direct search of the codebase for these routes, and a live Google Search Console check (batch_url_inspection) confirming all three are "Submitted and indexed," not missing or blocked.

The real finding is worse than "build it" and, fortunately, cheaper to fix: /areas/oakridge/ — the page that should be winning both Oakridge terms — had zero impressions in Google Search Console over the trailing 90 days, despite being indexed and already linked from the main nav, footer, homepage area grid, and the neighbourhood map. /blog/london-ontario-realtor/ had exactly one impression in 90 days, for "justin skrypnyk," not for its own target term — because literally no page on the site linked to it anywhere except its own listing in the blog index. The three "First changes" in Section 5 have been rewritten below to match what was actually shipped in response.

How the traffic estimates were calculated

Search volumes are from the API. The click figures apply an assumed organic click-through rate of 2.5 to 3.0 percent for a position 8 to 10 result, which is an industry benchmark and did not come from the API. Working: 2,400 x 0.025 to 0.030 = 60 to 72. 390 x 0.025 to 0.030 = 9.8 to 11.7. 170 x 0.025 to 0.030 = 4.3 to 5.1. Current traffic is deducted where measurable, which changes nothing here because current ETV totals 0.57 across the whole site. Treat these as order-of-magnitude figures, not forecasts.

What was unavailable, and why

  • Keyword Difficulty for the two ranking keywords. The API returned no difficulty value for "houses for sale oakridge london ontario" or "oakridge london ontario". Those cells read "not available" rather than carrying an estimate. Difficulty was returned for "london ontario realtor" (16).
  • City-level ranked-keyword data. The Labs ranked-keywords endpoint accepts country-level locations only, so the keyword table is Canada-wide. London-level positions came from live SERP calls, which is why the two sets of numbers differ.
  • Largest Contentful Paint. The on-page endpoint returned 0 for LCP on both pages tested, which is a null rather than a measurement, so no speed verdict is given. Time to interactive was returned: 1,081 ms for the homepage and 801 ms for the blog post.
  • Competitor page depth. cassidyrealestate.ca returned an HTTP 218 response with 53 words of text, so its real word count and structure could not be compared. No estimate was substituted.
  • One keyword with no data. "living in oakridge london ontario" was submitted to the keyword overview endpoint and returned nothing, meaning the database holds no record of it.
  • Stale CMS field. The backlinks endpoint reports the CMS as Wix. The live server header is Netlify, so that field reflects an older crawl and was not used in any conclusion.
